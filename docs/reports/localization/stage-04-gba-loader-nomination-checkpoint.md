## Demonstrated defects and narrow corrections

- **Ruby/Emerald:** the collector's lexical prefix can interpret switch-table or
  literal-pool data as instructions and stop before a complete local source /
  destination / call sequence. A finite self-contained argument-tail fallback
  repairs this without admitting managed-background graphics. Successful existing
  traces and their live-ins remain preferred.
- **FireRed:** the range-guard default edge preserves a symbolic RAM destination
  root, but the lexical prefix starts after an unrelated branch and loses it.
  A finite compiled guard-to-tail envelope recovers that root without reading RAM
  or traversing the other switch arms. Known conflicting arguments are never
  overwritten. An impossible out-of-range edge after an unsigned-byte comparison
  against its maximum is rejected.

These are diagnostic argument envelopes, not executable-entry, complete CFG,
callee-semantics, title or whole-original uniqueness proofs. Existing nomination,
byte, reference, time and caller ceilings remain unchanged. Malformed contenders
remain visible and cannot be removed to manufacture uniqueness.
